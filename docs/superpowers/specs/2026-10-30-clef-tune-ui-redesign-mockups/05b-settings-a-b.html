The Settings step

Two ways to lay out the same choices, with real estimates for the 200-ticket example. Change the model or the amount of training and watch the time and cost update. "Advanced settings" opens at the bottom.

A · Three side-by-side choices

The name, then the model, then three columns for how much training, each with its own time and cost so you can compare at a glance. The total sits underneath.

B · One sentence

The whole setup reads as a sentence. Each underlined word is a choice: click it for the alternatives, or type a new name. It's the calmest and shortest version, but the options are hidden until you click.
